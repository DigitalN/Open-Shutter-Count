Set up the camera

  • USB connection set to Photo Import/Remote Control
    On the camera: MENU › Setup (wrench) › Choose USB connection app. On the Canon R8 it's on page 4 of the Setup menu.
  • Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are off on your camera
  • A card in the camera
  • Plugged straight into this Mac with a data cable
    Not through a hub or dock. Some USB-C cables only charge.
  • Image Capture, Photos and EOS Utility closed

Looking for the camera…

Switch it on and plug it in. This moves on by itself as soon as it's found.

  • Is its USB connection set to Photo Import/Remote Control? MENU › Setup (wrench) › Choose USB connection app; on the Canon R8, page 4 of the Setup menu. In any other mode it can't be read.
  • Did macOS ask to allow the accessory? On a Mac laptop with Apple silicon, macOS asks before a new USB accessory can connect. Click Allow. If you clicked Don't Allow, unplug the camera and plug it back in to be asked again. The setting is in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Allow accessories to connect.
  • Perhaps try another USB cable? Not all cables are capable of reading data over USB. Try another high quality cable.
  • Are Wi-Fi and Bluetooth off on your camera? On some Canon bodies, when those are on, USB ports are disabled for data.

Reading the camera…

Don't unplug it. This takes a few seconds.
